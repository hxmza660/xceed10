Triangles

Master similarity, proportional sides and the Basic Proportionality Theorem.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Similar triangles have equal corresponding angles and proportional corresponding sides.
  • Basic Proportionality Theorem links a line parallel to one side with equal side ratios.
  • Use AA, SAS or SSS similarity criteria appropriately.
  • Once triangles are similar, corresponding side ratios and area ratios follow.
If ΔABC ~ ΔDEF, AB/DE = BC/EF = CA/FD and area ratio = (AB/DE)².

🎯 PYQ Pattern Practice

These are original Xceed10 board-style questions modeled on the kinds of skills assessed by CBSE — not copied past-paper questions.

1. State the Basic Proportionality Theorem.
Answer approach

A line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides in the same ratio.

2. If two triangles are similar in ratio 2:3, what is their area ratio?
Answer approach

4:9.

3. Which similarity criterion uses two equal angles?
Answer approach

AA.
