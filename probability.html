Probability

Use classical probability for simple events and interpret outcomes.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Probability lies between 0 and 1 inclusive.
  • For equally likely outcomes, P(E)=favourable outcomes/total outcomes.
  • Impossible event has probability 0; sure event has probability 1.
  • Count sample-space outcomes carefully and avoid double-counting.
P(E)=Number of favourable outcomes / Total number of equally likely outcomes.
