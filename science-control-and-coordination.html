Control and Coordination

Explain nervous coordination, reflexes, plant hormones and animal hormones.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • A reflex arc produces a quick automatic response.
  • Plants show tropic movements such as phototropism and geotropism.
  • Hormones act as chemical messengers; endocrine glands release them into blood.
  • Distinguish nervous control (fast) from hormonal control (slower, longer-lasting).
