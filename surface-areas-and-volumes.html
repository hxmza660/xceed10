Surface Areas and Volumes

Work with surface area and volume of combined solids.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Write each component’s formula before combining.
  • Check whether a surface is internal and should not be counted in exposed area.
  • Volume is additive/subtractive when solids are joined or hollowed.
  • Keep all dimensions in the same unit.
Cylinder V=πr²h; sphere V=4/3πr³; cone V=1/3πr²h; cuboid V=lbh.

🎯 PYQ Pattern Practice

These are original Xceed10 board-style questions modeled on the kinds of skills assessed by CBSE — not copied past-paper questions.

1. Find the volume of a cylinder with r=3 cm, h=10 cm.
Answer approach

90π cm³.

2. When is curved surface area used instead of total surface area?
Answer approach

When only the curved part is exposed/required.

3. What is a common unit error?
Answer approach

Mixing cm and m in the same calculation.
