🎯 PYQ Pattern Practice

These are original Xceed10 board-style questions modeled on the kinds of skills assessed by CBSE — not copied past-paper questions.

1. Explain one central theme of “Fire and Ice” and support it with one event or image from the text.
Answer approach

Write a 40–50 word evidence-based response using a specific incident or poetic image.

2. How does the title “Fire and Ice” connect to the text?
Answer approach

Link the title to the central conflict, character change or main image/theme.

3. What inference can a reader make about the main character or speaker?
Answer approach

State the inference and support it with one textual detail in your own words.
