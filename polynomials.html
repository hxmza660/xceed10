Polynomials

Connect zeros of polynomials with coefficients and graphs.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • A zero of p(x) is an x-value for which p(x)=0.
  • For ax²+bx+c, sum of zeros = −b/a and product = c/a.
  • Use factorisation or graph interpretation to identify zeros.
  • A quadratic can have two, one or no real zeros depending on its graph/intersections.
If zeros are α, β: α+β=−b/a; αβ=c/a.

🎯 PYQ Pattern Practice

These are original Xceed10 board-style questions modeled on the kinds of skills assessed by CBSE — not copied past-paper questions.

1. Find the zeros of x²−5x+6.
Answer approach

2 and 3.

2. If zeros of 2x²−kx+3 are 1 and 3/2, find k.
Answer approach

k = 5.

3. How are zeros related to intersections of a polynomial graph with the x-axis?
Answer approach

They are the x-coordinates of the intersection points.
