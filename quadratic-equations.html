Quadratic Equations

Solve quadratic equations by factorisation or formula and interpret the discriminant.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Standard form: ax²+bx+c=0 with a≠0.
  • Factorisation is quickest when integer factors are easy to spot.
  • The quadratic formula works for every quadratic with real coefficients.
  • The discriminant tells the nature of real roots.
x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac))/(2a); D>0: two real distinct roots; D=0: equal real roots; D<0: no real roots.

1. Solve x²−7x+12=0.
Answer approach

x=3,4.

2. What happens when the discriminant is zero?
Answer approach

The quadratic has two equal real roots.

3. Form a quadratic equation whose roots are 2 and −5.
Answer approach

x²+3x−10=0.
