Circles

Focus on tangents: perpendicular radius theorem and equal tangents from an external point.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • The tangent touches a circle at exactly one point.
  • The tangent at the point of contact is perpendicular to the radius through that point.
  • Tangents drawn from the same external point are equal in length.
  • Use right-triangle geometry with the radius when needed.
If PA and PB are tangents from P, then PA=PB.

🎯 PYQ Pattern Practice

These are original Xceed10 board-style questions modeled on the kinds of skills assessed by CBSE — not copied past-paper questions.

1. What angle does the radius make with the tangent at the point of contact?
Answer approach

90°.

2. If PA and PB are tangents from P and PA=7 cm, find PB.
Answer approach

7 cm.

3. Why are tangents useful in geometry proofs?
Answer approach

They create a right angle with the radius.
