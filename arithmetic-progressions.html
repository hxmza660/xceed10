Arithmetic Progressions

Recognise constant differences and calculate terms and sums of arithmetic sequences.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • An AP has a constant common difference d.
  • The nth term lets you jump directly to any position.
  • Use the sum formula when many terms are involved.
  • Translate word problems into first term, difference and number of terms before calculating.
aₙ=a+(n−1)d; Sₙ=n/2[2a+(n−1)d].
