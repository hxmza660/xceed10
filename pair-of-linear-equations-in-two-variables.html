Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

Solve two simultaneous linear equations graphically and algebraically.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • A pair can have one solution, no solution or infinitely many solutions.
  • Graphically, the intersection point gives the unique solution.
  • Use substitution or elimination carefully and check the answer in both equations.
  • For a₁x+b₁y+c₁=0 and a₂x+b₂y+c₂=0, compare coefficient ratios for consistency.
Unique: a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂. No solution: a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂. Infinite: a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂.

🎯 PYQ Pattern Practice

These are original Xceed10 board-style questions modeled on the kinds of skills assessed by CBSE — not copied past-paper questions.

1. Solve x+y=7 and x−y=1.
Answer approach

x=4, y=3.

2. When do two linear equations have infinitely many solutions?
Answer approach

When all three coefficient ratios are equal.

3. Why is checking the final ordered pair useful?
Answer approach

It catches algebraic or sign errors.
