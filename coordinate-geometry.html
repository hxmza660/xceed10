Coordinate Geometry

Use coordinates to measure distance and divide a line segment internally.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Distance formula comes from Pythagoras.
  • Section formula gives the coordinates of an internal division point in a stated ratio.
  • Keep x- and y-coordinates paired with the correct ratio terms.
  • Sketching a quick coordinate diagram reduces sign mistakes.
Distance = √[(x₂−x₁)²+(y₂−y₁)²]. Section (m:n) = ((mx₂+nx₁)/(m+n),(my₂+ny₁)/(m+n)).

3. What is the main error to avoid in section formula?
