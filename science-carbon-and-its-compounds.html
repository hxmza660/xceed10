Carbon and its Compounds

Understand carbon’s tetravalency, catenation, hydrocarbons and key reactions.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Saturated compounds have single bonds; unsaturated compounds contain double/triple bonds.
  • Homologous series have the same functional group and a regular increase in molecular mass.
  • Addition is typical for unsaturated compounds; substitution is common in saturated hydrocarbons.
  • Know core properties and uses of ethanol and ethanoic acid, plus soaps and detergents.
