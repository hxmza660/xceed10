Real Numbers

Build the number-system foundation: prime factorisation, irrationality proofs and the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Every composite number has a unique prime factorisation apart from the order of factors.
  • Use prime powers to reason about HCF and LCM.
  • For irrationality proofs, assume the number is rational and write it in lowest form before deriving a contradiction.
  • Know how decimal expansions relate to prime factors of a denominator.
For two positive integers a,b: HCF(a,b) × LCM(a,b) = a × b.

🎯 PYQ Pattern Practice

These are original Xceed10 board-style questions modeled on the kinds of skills assessed by CBSE — not copied past-paper questions.

1. Use prime factorisation to find the HCF and LCM of 84 and 126.
Answer approach

HCF = 42; LCM = 252.

2. Explain the core contradiction used to prove √2 is irrational.
Answer approach

Assuming √2 = p/q in lowest terms leads to both p and q being even.

3. What condition on prime factors of q makes p/q have a terminating decimal?
Answer approach

After reduction, q has only 2 and/or 5 as prime factors.
