Some Applications to Trigonometry

Use elevation/depression and standard-angle ratios to solve heights and distances.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Angle of elevation is measured upward from the horizontal; angle of depression is measured downward.
  • Draw the right triangle first, then label known sides and angle.
  • Use only the ratio that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  • For standard Class 10 problems, angles are commonly 30°,45°,60°.
tan θ = opposite/adjacent is often the fastest tool for height-distance problems.

🎯 PYQ Pattern Practice

These are original Xceed10 board-style questions modeled on the kinds of skills assessed by CBSE — not copied past-paper questions.

1. A tower is viewed at 45° from a point 20 m away. Ignoring observer height, find the tower height.
Answer approach

20 m.

2. What is an angle of depression?
Answer approach

The angle between the horizontal and the line of sight when looking downward.

3. Why should you draw the diagram first?
Answer approach

It prevents incorrect identification of opposite/adjacent sides.
