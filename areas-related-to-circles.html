Areas Related to Circles

Calculate sectors, segments, arc lengths and perimeters using central angles.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Sector area is the required fraction of the full circle based on the central angle.
  • Segment area = sector area − area of the corresponding triangle.
  • Keep units consistent before substituting values.
  • For the current syllabus, segment problems use restricted standard central angles.
Sector area = θ/360 × πr²; arc length = θ/360 × 2πr.

🎯 PYQ Pattern Practice

These are original Xceed10 board-style questions modeled on the kinds of skills assessed by CBSE — not copied past-paper questions.

1. Find the area of a 90° sector of radius 14 cm using π=22/7.
Answer approach

154 cm².

2. What is a minor sector?
Answer approach

The smaller sector formed by a central angle less than 180°.

3. How do you get segment area?
Answer approach

Subtract the triangle area from the sector area.
