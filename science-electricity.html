Electricity

Relate current, potential difference, resistance, power and circuit combinations.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Ohm’s law: V=IR for an ohmic conductor at constant temperature.
  • Series resistors add directly; parallel combinations reduce equivalent resistance.
  • Power can be P=VI=I²R=V²/R.
  • Electricity bills commonly involve energy used in kWh.
