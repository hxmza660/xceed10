Acids, Bases and Salts

Connect ions, indicators, pH, neutralisation and common salts to everyday uses.

⭐ Ace Notes🎯 PYQ Pattern

⭐ Ace Notes

  • Acids furnish H+ in water; bases furnish OH−.
  • Indicators reveal acidic/basic nature; pH below 7 is acidic and above 7 basic.
  • Neutralisation is acid + base → salt + water.
  • Know uses/preparation ideas of NaOH, bleaching powder, baking soda, washing soda and POP.
